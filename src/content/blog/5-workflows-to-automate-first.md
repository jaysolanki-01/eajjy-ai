Most firms know they *should* automate, but freeze at the question of where to start. The trick is to begin where the time leak is biggest and the process is most repetitive. Here are the five workflows we almost always tackle first.

## 1. Client onboarding

Onboarding is death by a thousand manual steps: collecting documents, creating folders, sending welcome emails, provisioning tool access. A single automated workflow can turn a four-hour process into a twenty-minute one.

- Auto-generate intake forms and sync responses to your CRM
- Trigger folder creation and document requests automatically
- Send a branded welcome sequence without lifting a finger

## 2. Reporting

If someone on your team spends hours each week copying numbers into a deck, that's a workflow begging to be automated. Pull data from every platform, assemble the report, and schedule delivery.

> Teams typically recover 6–10 hours a week just by automating recurring reports.

## 3. Email triage

AI can read, categorize, and draft responses to routine email, so your team only touches what genuinely needs a human.

## 4. Document processing

Invoices, contracts, and forms can be parsed, categorized, and routed with near-perfect accuracy, eliminating manual data entry entirely.

## 5. Lead follow-up

Every lead that slips through the cracks is lost revenue. Automated scoring and routing make sure the right person follows up at the right time, every time.
