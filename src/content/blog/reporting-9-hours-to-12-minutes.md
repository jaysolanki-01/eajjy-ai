One of our clients, a mid-sized consulting firm, had a standing Friday ritual nobody enjoyed: pulling numbers from four different tools into a single client report. It ate most of an afternoon, every week.

## The problem

Every Friday, an analyst would:

1. Export data from the CRM, the billing system, the project tracker, and a spreadsheet
2. Reconcile mismatched formats by hand
3. Paste everything into a templated deck
4. Proofread and send

Total time: around **9 hours a week**, and the occasional copy-paste error slipping through.

## What we built

We designed a single automated pipeline that connects all four sources, normalizes the data, and assembles the report on a schedule.

> The analyst now spends about 12 minutes reviewing a report that builds itself.

## The result

- **9 hours a week** recovered, roughly 450 hours a year
- Zero reconciliation errors since launch
- The analyst moved onto actual client strategy work

The numbers are nice. What the client actually valued was getting a skilled person off a robotic task and back onto work that grows the business.
