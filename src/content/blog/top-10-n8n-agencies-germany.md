## What to look for in an n8n agency

Before we get to the list, it's worth being clear about what separates a good n8n agency from a freelancer with a portfolio. When you're building automation that touches your CRM, your financial data, or your client-facing workflows, you need more than someone who can connect two apps.

**Production experience.** Anyone can build a workflow that works in testing. Fewer have built systems that run reliably at scale, with proper error handling, monitoring, and failover logic.

**Integration depth.** N8n connects to hundreds of platforms, but the hard part is understanding how your specific stack — your CRM, your ERP, your communication tools — actually behaves in edge cases. Look for agencies with documented experience connecting the specific tools you use.

**Business understanding.** The best n8n agencies don't just execute technical specs — they help you figure out which workflows are worth automating and in what order. That requires business judgment, not just code.

**Documentation and handover.** You should never be locked into an agency. Every workflow should be documented clearly enough that you can understand it, modify it, or hand it to someone else.

**Support model.** What happens when a workflow breaks at 11pm before a critical deadline? Understanding the agency's response SLA and support model is essential before you sign.

## How to choose the right n8n agency in Germany

With ten strong options, the choice comes down to a few key factors specific to your situation.

### Match the agency to your industry

The agencies on this list have very different sector strengths. Flowcraft Digital understands SAP; Prozesswerk understands financial compliance; DataBridge understands factory floors. Choosing an agency with documented experience in your industry means they already understand the edge cases, data structures, and regulatory constraints you'll encounter. A generalist agency will spend the first weeks learning what an industry-specialist already knows.

### Consider your timeline

Some agencies (particularly the specialist ones in Munich and Frankfurt) operate on longer engagement cycles — 3 to 6 months minimum, with structured phases. Others (Logikraft, EAJJY AI) are set up for faster, fixed-scope builds. If you need something live in six weeks, be upfront about that in your initial conversation and choose accordingly.

### Ask about their monitoring and support model

N8n workflows can break — an API changes, a third-party service goes down, a data format shifts. The question isn't whether something will go wrong but how quickly it gets fixed when it does. Ask specifically: what does monitoring look like? What's the response time when a production workflow fails? Is there a support retainer option?

### Request documentation examples

Before signing with any agency, ask to see an example of the documentation they deliver with a project. A workflow diagram alone isn't sufficient — you want to see logic documentation that explains what the workflow does, what triggers it, what happens when it fails, and how to modify the key variables. This tells you immediately whether the agency is building for the long term or for the handover.

### Get a fixed quote, not hourly

Hourly engagements for automation projects tend to run over budget because scope is genuinely hard to estimate in advance. Look for agencies willing to scope the project first (usually a discovery phase) and then provide a fixed quote for the build. This aligns incentives and protects you from open-ended billing.

## Questions to ask before hiring an n8n agency

Use these in your first conversation with any agency on this list:

1. **Can you show me a live example of a production n8n workflow you've built?** (Not a demo — something actually running for a client)
2. **How do you handle error handling and what does monitoring look like in practice?**
3. **What happens to our data if we stop working with you?** (All workflows, credentials, and documentation should be fully portable)
4. **Do you self-host n8n or do you use n8n Cloud for your clients?** (Self-hosted is more flexible and avoids per-execution pricing)
5. **What's your handover process, and could our internal team modify the workflows after delivery?**

Any agency confident in their work will answer these without hesitation. Vague answers to any of them are a signal to keep looking.

## FAQ

### What does an n8n agency in Germany typically cost?

Project-based engagements typically range from €5,000 to €50,000 depending on scope and complexity. Ongoing retainers for maintenance and development usually run €1,500 to €6,000 per month. Agencies in major German cities (Berlin, Munich, Frankfurt) tend to have higher day rates than those in smaller cities or remote-first agencies.

### Is n8n suitable for large German enterprises, or is it mainly for SMEs?

N8n is used by organisations of all sizes, from solo founders to enterprises with thousands of employees. Its self-hosting capability, enterprise licence, and support for complex logic make it genuinely enterprise-grade. Several agencies on this list work exclusively with mid-market and enterprise clients. The limiting factor is usually finding the right implementation partner rather than the platform itself.

### Should I use n8n Cloud or self-host n8n?

For most German businesses with data privacy concerns, self-hosting is preferable because it keeps your data within your own infrastructure and jurisdiction — important for GDPR compliance. N8n Cloud is faster to set up and eliminates server management, but execution data lives on n8n's infrastructure. Ask your chosen agency which they recommend based on your specific compliance requirements.

### How long does a typical n8n automation project take?

A single workflow (e.g. automating client onboarding or lead follow-up) typically takes 2–4 weeks from scoping to deployment. A broader automation programme covering multiple workflows can take 3–6 months. Agencies that skip the discovery phase tend to take longer overall because they encounter unexpected complexity mid-build.

### Can I switch n8n agencies if I'm not happy with the first one?

Yes, provided your workflows are documented and accessible. N8n workflows are exportable as JSON files, and a well-run agency will give you full access to everything they've built. The key question to ask upfront is who owns the credentials, workflow files, and documentation — the answer should always be you.
